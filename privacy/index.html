Privacy Policy

Nothing phones home.

MadChat is built by theMad as a native Apple client for Delta Chat, Matrix, and XMPP. We collect no personal data, run no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and operate no MadChat relay servers.

1. Zero telemetry & zero tracking

MadChat does not collect usage telemetry, advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, analytics events, or crash reports. No third-party tracking or advertising SDKs are linked into the macOS or iOS applications.

2. Your servers, your messages

When you sign in to a Delta Chat (chatmail / IMAP / SMTP), Matrix, or XMPP account, MadChat connects directly to the servers you configure. Message contents, attachments, contacts, and account credentials stay locally on your device (protected by the system sandbox and Apple Keychain) and are transmitted only to the messaging servers you chose and the contacts or rooms you communicate with.

3. Optional encrypted iCloud Sync & Backups

If you choose to turn on iCloud Sync or back up chat history in Settings, MadChat encrypts your account bundle or history archive on-device using a sync password you choose before saving it to your private Apple iCloud CloudKit database (iCloud.org.themad.madchat). We never have access to your sync password or your iCloud data.

4. Opt-in network features

Optional GIF search (powered by Tenor) is disabled by default and asks for your explicit permission before making any network request. WebRTC audio/video calls and interactive webxdc mini-apps run only when you initiate or open them.

5. System permissions

Camera, microphone, photo library, and location permissions are requested only when you choose to scan a QR code, make a call, record voice messages, pick recent photos, or share your location in a chat.

6. Contact

Questions or privacy inquiries can be sent to madchat@themadorg.net or opened on GitHub Issues.
