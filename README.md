# MadChat

Native Apple messenger for **Delta Chat**, **Matrix**, and **XMPP** (with OMEMO) on **macOS 26** and **iOS 26** — built cleanly in Swift and Rust with zero telemetry.

- **Website:** https://themadorg.net/madchat/
- **Downloads (macOS DMG):** [Latest Release (v0.2.2)](https://github.com/themadorg/madchat/releases/tag/v0.2.2)
- **iOS & macOS Beta:** [Join TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/W6UbzUpp)
- **Privacy Policy:** https://themadorg.net/madchat/privacy/
- **Support:** https://themadorg.net/madchat/support/ · [Issues](https://github.com/themadorg/madchat/issues) · `madchat@themadorg.net`

## Highlights in v0.2.2

- **Three networks in one native binary:** Delta Chat (passwordless chatmail, QR SecureJoin, webxdc mini-apps), Matrix (E2EE rooms, spaces, directory), and native Swift XMPP (OMEMO encryption, presence, MUC).
- **Per-account colour identity:** Distinct palette washes across sidebar, selections, bubbles, and badges with verified contrast across light and dark modes.
- **Encrypted iCloud Sync & Continuity:** On-device encrypted account sync & backups via private Apple CloudKit container (`iCloud.org.themad.madchat`), second-device QR linking, and local `.tar` history export/restore across signed DMGs, App Store, and TestFlight.
- **Native desktop & mobile ergonomics:** Floating Liquid Glass in-chat search with live matching, scrollable multi-photo attach popover, live location sharing & map inspection, Notification Center & unread-aware menu-bar tray, RTL/Farsi & Arabic bubble alignment, and nested vector delivery checkmarks.

## Requirements

- **macOS:** macOS 26 or later (Apple Silicon)
- **iOS:** iOS 26 or later
