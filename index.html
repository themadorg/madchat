Three networks, one binary

One native app for the networks you already use

Delta Chat, Matrix, and XMPP live side by side in the same compiled Swift app — no bridge server, no hosted relay account, and no Electron shell.

Delta Chat

chatmail/core (Rust FFI)

Private and instant over email infrastructure. Mint a passwordless chatmail profile without a phone number, verify contacts with a QR code, and run webxdc apps inside chats.

  • Passwordless chatmail
  • QR SecureJoin
  • webxdc apps

Matrix

matrix-rust-sdk

Federated rooms, spaces, and user directory on any homeserver you choose. Every screen asks the account what it supports rather than assuming a single network.

  • Any homeserver
  • User directory
  • Rooms & spaces

XMPP

Native Swift client

The classic open messaging standard, compiled into the same app alongside Delta Chat and Matrix — not bridged through a third-party server.

  • Direct Jabber sign-in
  • Presence support
  • No bridge server
Built around what each network can actually do

A Delta Chat profile offers QR invites and passwordless chatmail; a Matrix account offers a homeserver user directory; an XMPP account shows presence when the server reports it. MadChat queries each account’s capabilities instead of showing broken controls.

Native before consistent

Two Apple platforms, each in its own idiom

macOS and iOS share the same Swift core and design rules, then diverge deliberately where each platform expects its own ergonomics.

macOS

macOS 26+ · Apple Silicon

Floating account rail and chat list beside an edge-to-edge conversation canvas. Open a dedicated window for a single account or a single chat, keep a menu-bar tray across full-screen spaces, and find messages with Spotlight.

  • Floating Liquid Glass panels & header capsules
  • Full, per-account, and per-chat window modes
  • Menu-bar tray & Spotlight indexing
  • Trackpad haptics & keyboard-first navigation

iOS

iOS 26+

Large-title navigation, a floating bottom tab bar within thumb reach, and a standalone search button. Every interactive control meets 44pt touch targets, with interruptible spring gestures and Dynamic Type.

  • Floating bottom tab bar: Contacts, Chats, Profile
  • 44pt touch targets & thumb-reach search
  • Bottom sheets, swipe actions & tactile haptics
  • Full Dynamic Type & Reduce Motion support

Truth over reassurance

The green seal means verified — nothing less

MadChat never shows an app-wide “everything is encrypted” lock. Trust is verified per contact, and the green seal appears only beside people you have actually verified.

Nothing phones home

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash trackers. Network reach is a feature you turn on — even Tenor GIF search stays off until you explicitly opt in.

No hosted account

Your identity lives on servers you choose. There is no MadChat relay account, no mandatory cloud sync, and no proprietary lock-in.

Plain, honest verbs

Deleting or clearing a chat stays local. Leaving a group is observable to members and labelled as such. Unknown connectivity says unknown.
